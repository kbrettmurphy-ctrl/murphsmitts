Full Relace

What a full glove relace includes

A full relace means removing the old lace and replacing it through the web, fingers, palm, heel, and wrist areas. It is different from fixing one broken section.

New lace throughout

Old lace is removed and replaced across the glove, so tension is more consistent from the web to the heel.

Structure and pocket support

Fresh lace helps the glove hold shape, close cleanly, and keep the web and fingers from shifting during normal play.

Color and service fit

You can stay close to the original lace color or choose a different look. If the glove only needs targeted work, I’ll say so.

Lace Condition

When a glove needs relacing

A glove likely needs attention when the lace is dry, cracked, stretched, missing, or no longer holding tension. Loose fingers, a collapsing pocket, or a shifting web are also signs to look at.

Common signs

  • Broken or brittle lace.
  • Loose web, fingers, heel, or wrist.
  • Pocket shape changing more than it should.
  • Lace that stretches instead of holding tension.

How I review it

Photos help me see the lace, leather condition, pocket, and structure before the glove comes in. I’ll recommend the work that makes sense, whether that is a full relace, targeted lace repair, cleaning and conditioning, or a smaller fix.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about timing, glove types, mail-in service, pricing, and lace choices.

How long does glove relacing take?

Turnaround depends on my current workload, but most relaces are completed within 1-2 weeks once I have the glove and the service plan is confirmed. I’ll follow up after you submit the service request.

Can you relace a catcher’s mitt or first base mitt?

Yes. Catcher’s mitts, first base mitts, trapeze webs, and modified trapeze webs are all welcome. You can also view examples in the gallery.

Do you offer mail-in glove repair?

Yes. Mail-in service is available. Start with the request form so I can review the glove and send the right shipping instructions before it is sent in.

Is it worth relacing an older glove?

Often, yes. If the leather shell is still sound, new lace can bring back structure and pocket tension without replacing the glove. If the leather is too far gone or outside the work I can responsibly do, I’ll tell you before the work starts.

What do you use to clean gloves?

I use saddle soap and glove care products from The Craftsman’s Works for cleaning and conditioning.

Can I choose my lace color?

Yes. You can review available options in the lace color guide and include your preference on the service request.

How much does a full glove relace cost?

Pricing depends on glove type, web style, condition, and the work needed. Standard fielding gloves differ from catcher’s mitts and first base mitts because they usually require more lace and labor. Current pricing is listed on the services page.
