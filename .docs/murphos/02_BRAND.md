# Brand Identity

Murph's Mitts exists to bring baseball and softball gloves back to life by extending the same level of care to every part of the customer experience.

Every glove carries a story. Years of games. Practices. Championships. Missed opportunities. Countless catches. Those marks aren't flaws to erase. They're part of what makes every glove unique.

Murph's Mitts exists to preserve that history while restoring a glove's feel, structure, and readiness for the next game through thoughtful care and exceptional craftsmanship.

The goal isn't to erase a glove's history.

The goal is to preserve its character while restoring its feel, structure, and readiness for its next chapter.

Customers trust us with more than leather.

They trust us with memories.

Everything we do should honor that trust.

---

# Brand Promise

Every glove that comes through Murph's Mitts will be treated with the same care, attention, and respect we'd expect for our own.

Our responsibility extends beyond the workbench. From the first visit to the website to the final knot, every interaction should reflect the same craftsmanship and attention to detail that goes into the glove itself.

# Our Values

## Care Above All

Everything begins with care.

The glove.

The customer.

The experience.

No detail is too small.

---

## We Are Caretakers

Every glove that arrives at Murph's Mitts is entrusted to our care.

For a short time, it leaves the dugout, the equipment bag, and the backseat of a truck. It receives the attention it deserves. It's cleaned thoughtfully, conditioned carefully, relaced with quality materials, and prepared for many more seasons of play.

Our job isn't to erase the glove's history.

It's to honor it, care for it, and return it ready for its next chapter.

Being trusted with someone's glove is a responsibility, not a transaction.

---

## Honor Character

Every glove tells a story.

Signs of use are not flaws.

They're reminders of games played, seasons completed, and memories made.

Murph's Mitts preserves that character while extending the glove's life through thoughtful care and quality craftsmanship.

Patina isn't something to remove.

It's something to respect.

---

## Craftsmanship Extends Beyond the Glove

The quality of our work doesn't end when the last lace is tied.

It should be reflected in every email, every text message, every page of the website, every package, and every customer interaction.

The experience deserves the same level of craftsmanship as the glove.
