# MurphOS Design Language

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Locked
**Last Updated:** June 2026

---

# Why This Exists

Murph's Mitts restores baseball and softball gloves with exceptional craftsmanship and an unwavering respect for the customer.

Every glove carries a story. A first home run. A state championship. A season that ended too soon. A parent preserving a childhood memory. Customers don't send us leather. They send us something they care about.

MurphOS exists to ensure every part of that experience reflects the same level of care that's given to every glove.

The website.

The order tracker.

The emails.

The text messages.

The admin tools.

The packaging.

Every interaction should feel calm.

Every page should feel intentional.

Every detail should communicate craftsmanship.

The interface is never the product.

The glove is.

MurphOS exists to remove friction so the craftsmanship can take center stage.

---

# The MurphOS Philosophy

## Respect the Customer

Every click, every sentence, every animation, and every interaction either respects the customer's time or wastes it.

Choose respect.

Confusion is disrespect.

Unnecessary steps are disrespect.

Clutter is disrespect.

Good design is customer service.

---

## Respect the Glove

The glove is always the focal point.

The interface exists to frame the craftsmanship, never compete with it.

When in doubt, make the glove more important and the interface less noticeable.

---

## Sweat the Details

Customers may never notice perfectly aligned spacing, consistent typography, or smooth transitions.

They will notice how the experience makes them feel.

Quality is often invisible.

That's exactly the point.

---

## Quiet Confidence

Murph's Mitts never needs to shout.

Trust is earned through clarity, consistency, craftsmanship, and restraint.

Not oversized buttons.

Not flashy animations.

Not visual noise.

Confidence is communicated through thoughtful execution.

---

## Reduce Friction

Every design decision should accomplish at least one of the following:

* Reduce friction.
* Build trust.
* Showcase craftsmanship.

If it does none of those things, it doesn't belong.

---

## Build for the Long Term

Trends come and go.

Good design ages gracefully.

Every decision should still feel appropriate years after it's made.

Build systems, not trends.

Choose timeless over fashionable.

---

# Design Principles

Every part of MurphOS should embody these characteristics.

* Calm
* Intentional
* Consistent
* Honest
* Warm
* Precise
* Modern
* Accessible
* Human

MurphOS should never feel loud, rushed, cluttered, or over-designed.

---

# The Murph Test

Before adding, changing, or removing anything, ask:

1. Does this make the customer's experience easier?
2. Does this build trust?
3. Does this showcase the craftsmanship instead of competing with it?
4. Can this be simpler?
5. Can this be quieter?
6. Will this still feel right five years from now?

If the answer to most of these questions is no, rethink the solution.

---

# The Standard

MurphOS is not designed to impress.

It is designed to reassure.

Customers should never have to think about the interface.

They should simply feel that everything works exactly as it should.

When MurphOS succeeds, the technology fades into the background and the craftsmanship takes center stage.

That is the standard.

That is MurphOS.
